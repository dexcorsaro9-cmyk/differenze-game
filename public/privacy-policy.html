Paititi 1928: The Lost Expedition

Official Privacy Policy & Data Protection Statement
Effective Date: September 17, 2026 | Version 2.0

🛡️ Privacy-First Guarantee: Zero Personal Data Collection

Paititi 1928 is designed and developed with an offline-first, privacy-by-design architecture. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) or device telemetry. All game progression remains 100% on your local device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

When you play Paititi 1928: The Lost Expedition:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, address, or age.
  • No Device Tracking or Fingerprinting: We do not collect Advertising IDs (IDFA, AAID), IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, or IP addresses.
  • No Location Data: We never access GPS, cellular location, or network location.
  • No Audio or Camera Access: The game does not access your microphone, camera, photos, or contacts.
  • No Analytics or Third-Party SDKs: We do not embed Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook Pixel, or tracking SDKs.

2. Local Storage and Data Retention

The application saves data strictly within your device's private sandbox (using browser localStorage / Capacitor native storage) solely for core gameplay mechanics:

  • Expedition Progress: Completed chapters, star ratings, discovered differences, and best completion times.
  • Economy & Inventory: In-game gold coins, magnifying glass hints, time freezes, explorer visas, and unlocked relics.
  • Customization: Explorer avatar choices (Mateo / Samira), outfits, and narrative branch decisions.
  • Preferences: Audio volume, orchestral music toggle, vibration/haptic feedback, and interface language (IT, EN, ES).

Data Ownership & Deletion: You have absolute control over this data. You can export your full save data as a JSON file, import it to another device, or completely erase all progress at any moment using the "Reset Spedizione" button in the Settings menu or by clearing app data / uninstalling the application.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Our game complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Article 8 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

  • We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Union).
  • There are no behavioural advertising trackers or predatory monetization mechanics targeting minors.
  • The application is family-friendly and safe for explorers of all ages.

4. Device Permissions Explained

The application may request the following benign permissions solely for immersive sensory gameplay:

  • Vibration / Haptic Feedback (VIBRATE): Used exclusively to deliver subtle tactile feedback when tapping differences, finding clues, or solving puzzles. Can be toggled off at any time in Settings.
  • Network State (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used only by the browser Service Worker to verify offline status and load cached historical illustrations without network latency.

5. Third-Party Links & Distribution Platforms

The application is distributed via authorized app platforms (Google Play Store, Apple App Store, or as an installable Progressive Web App). These platforms operate under their respective privacy policies regarding app downloads and operating system updates. Paititi 1928 does not transmit in-game activity back to any external servers.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy to reflect future game enhancements or regulatory requirements. Any revisions will be published directly within this page with an updated "Effective Date".
